Executive Profile • Systems Narrative

Crafting software as architecture.

Aarav Sharma is a full-stack engineer and tech founder building high-performance web systems, low-latency microservices, and AI integrations from India.

"True engineering discipline is not about decorative complexity — it is about structural clarity. Code must load instantaneously, scale deterministically, and withstand production stress with zero excuses."

I am Aarav Sharma. Over the past several years, I have architected and deployed production digital products serving hundreds of thousands of users. My career bridges low-level backend engineering with pixel-precise, hardware-accelerated interfaces.

To channel this passion for engineering excellence, I founded two dedicated studios:

• Zephyr Devs: A specialized software and technology consulting studio delivering custom cloud backends, enterprise systems, and AI model orchestration for forward-thinking companies.

• Vortex Apps: A consumer product lab focused on low-latency audio engines (such as BeatWave) and high-speed cryptographic messaging layers (such as Onyx).

My engineering stack spans Kotlin and Node.js for distributed backends, coupled with React, Android Compose, and strict modern CSS for fluid user interfaces.
