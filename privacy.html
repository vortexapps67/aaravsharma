Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Authored by Aarav Sharma (Founder of Zephyr Devs & Vortex Apps).

1. Zero Telemetry Commitment

This website and applications authored by Aarav Sharma (including BeatWave and Onyx) adhere to strict data minimalism. We do not operate third-party tracking beacons, cross-site trackers, or advertising data brokers.

This site uses standard browser localStorage solely to remember your aesthetic theme preferences (dark obsidian or titanium light mode). No personal identifiers are stored or transmitted.

Information transmitted through the contact form is sent directly to Aarav Sharma for professional correspondence and project discussions. It is never sold, leased, or distributed to outside parties.
